Tsydon DM

An AI co-DM and DM training tool, built as a private project. This page is a status snapshot generated on 2026-10-27. It holds no studio, no campaign data and no secrets.

What it is

Tsydon DM listens to a tabletop game and whispers to the human DM only when it has something worth saying: a contradiction with something established earlier, a rule, a player left out, a way to improvise. Silence is the default and the DM stays in charge; nothing it says is canon until the DM makes it so. It also coaches DMs through practice tables with AI players.

What it does not do yet

  • Everything measured so far is on practice tables or on slices of two recorded sessions from two groups.
  • Who spoke on a recording is guessed from the words and has not been checked against a person.
  • The cue judge is a model, not a DM; a person's reading of the cues is the check that counts.
  • Debrief-quality scores are comparable only when the same judge produced them: rows scored by the direct-API judge (names starting rs-api, and iter-025-api) are not comparable with the default judge, which scored the same debriefs higher in every test.
  • There is no live audio, no Discord and no always-on connection, and no plan to host the studio publicly.
